# Weekly Vault Synthesis

1. Confirm the vault root. Read material added in the last 7 days across `00-INBOX`, `01-CAPTURES`, `02-CONNECTIONS`, `03-BRIEFS`, and `1on1s`.
2. Produce four evidence-backed sections:
   - **EMERGING THESIS:** The idea or position forming in the user's thinking.
   - **CONTRADICTIONS:** Recent material that challenges earlier beliefs, with both sides quoted.
   - **KNOWLEDGE GAPS:** Missing perspectives or sources the user should consider.
   - **ONE ACTION:** The single highest-leverage thing to do or think about this week.
3. Be direct and challenge assumptions. Do not summarize familiar material without adding an insight.
4. Save the result to `02-CONNECTIONS/synthesis-YYYY-MM-DD.md` and print the complete synthesis.

Do not fabricate evidence, quotations, or file citations.
